## 8. 最终结论与 caveat（建议写入产物的准确措辞）

**Conclusions**

1. **Engineering: VALID.** Across all 22 authorized Phase 7 starts there is no invalid/error artifact, no reset-invariant failure, no orphan/lease residue, no fabricated inactive-counter zero, and no natural cross-store reservation failure. All A8 same-context canaries matched.
2. **Mechanism (A8 R0 ceiling): NEGATIVE.** At the primary configuration `chunked_prefill_size=4096 / max_prefill_tokens=4096`, the paired-target request-path median speedup is 0.7723, 0.7751, 0.9334 and 0.9362 across the four restart-0 settings — R0 is 6–23% *slower* than dense, i.e. opposite in sign to the preregistered +5% MDE. The N=8 full-setup amortized speedup is 0.6086–0.6419 and `break_even_observed_N` is `>8/not_observed` in every repeat. **No mechanism-intrinsic speedup headline is published.** The result rests on **one process-level replicate per setting**; ES-R0-MDE correctly skipped the 8 supplement starts, so the preregistered 3-replicate range could not be reported.
3. **chunk1024 sensitivity is chunk-coupled and is not a headline.** The restart-median request-path speedup is 1.7370 and the N=8 full-setup median is 1.1890, versus 0.9362 and 0.6419 for the same body/rho at chunk4096. The ~1.85x swing is attributable to the coupled `chunked_prefill_size`/`max_prefill_tokens` change, not to the recovery mechanism.
4. **System behaviour (W, S4 vs S0): INCONCLUSIVE / DESCRIPTIVE.** Median over three independent server restarts: at rho=1.5, S0/S4 all-reusable TTFT mean ratio 1.0021, workflow-only 1.0276, S4−S0 all-reusable miss delta −14, R0 peak ratio S4/S0 1.0112; at rho=2.0, 1.0250, 1.0442, +2 and 0.9981. The preregistered stop rule (§8.7-5) did not fire, but §8.5 forbids a practical-benefit claim regardless, and latency is secondary after A8 NEGATIVE. **No winner is selected and no scheduler performance claim is published.**
5. **R4-like is a synthetic 5x footprint proxy and executes no KVCOMM.** S0 completed the 61-request trace in both repeats (`available`, artifact `valid`); S4 failed registration in both repeats (`diagnostic_unavailable`, artifact `inconclusive`). Performance ranking is disabled.
6. **Budget.** 22 of 30 committed starts executed in 1.31014 GPU-equivalent hours (34.5% of the 3.8 h expectation, 21.8% of the 6.0 h hard cap); wall-clock span 1.52884 h. Zero retries.

**Caveats（必须与结论同时出现）**

1. `practical=NONE` is scoped to this model, synthetic prompt family, exact-output invariant, this GPU and `chunk=1024`; it is not a general infeasibility claim.
2. R0 is a ceiling path, not a promoted practical candidate.
3. R2 is `disabled_not_comparable`; Phase 4 chunk1024 R2 numbers must never be ranked or pooled with Phase 7 chunk4096 results.
4. `r2_like` is a 2x synthetic representation-multiplicity footprint profile, not R2 execution. `R4-like-5x` is a synthetic footprint proxy, not KVCOMM.
5. P6-F proves fault-injected fallback only. Natural-pressure fallback reachability remains **unproven**: both wave-0 cells report `fallback_reachability {passed:false, rounds:0}` and zero natural reservation failures were observed in any of the 22 starts.
6. In wave-0, "reachable" means the profile registered and the round passed its validity/reset checks; it does **not** mean approximate recovery succeeded. S0 recorded `approximate_gpu_recovery=0` and `dense_fallback=5/5` in every approximate profile and both repeats — the reason its cell is `diagnostic-unavailable` and the artifact is `inconclusive`. S4 recorded 5/5 recoveries in the four non-R4 profiles.
7. In the W matrix the R0 arm falls back to dense on 45.9%–72.1% of requests (terminal reason `unsupported` ← raw `store_miss`); the S4-vs-S0 latency ratios therefore compare arms that are substantially dense-fallback. The R4-like S0 diagnostic recovered only 12/122 requests (9.8%).
8. The independent replicate unit is the server restart. Requests within a trace are **not** independent timing replicates; request-level bootstrap is forbidden. Reported p95 quantities are ratios of marginal p95s, not paired statistics. W uses 3 restarts; A8 primary and R4-like use 1; chunk1024 sensitivity uses 2.
9. host / prefetch / async / HiCache tracks were not generated in V7 and their counters were asserted inactive only.
10. P6-4 rho1.1 / rho1.5 / rho3 feasibility remains chunk1024-scoped unless separately revalidated; no chunk4096/rho3 claim is permitted.
