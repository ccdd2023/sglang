=== DIAGNOSTIC VERDICT (Phase 6 device-resident, 2026-07-02) ===
Answer to "假设我们全部都提前放到GPU上，忽略传输时间，能加速多少？":
**NEGATIVE — 0× speedup. Device-resident 960ms is SLOWER than lossless 948ms
and slower than host-pool SYNC 923ms.** With zero H2D transfer cost (KV already
on GPU), the reuse mechanism itself does NOT deliver a speedup at this scale
(867-tok reuse on a ~1500-tok prefill ≈ 50% reuse rate). The bottleneck is
NOT transfer — it's the read-path itself: move_kv_cache gather-scatter +
default-stream RoPE rewrite + alloc overhead consume the saved prefill time.

Implications:
- Speed path: a different mechanism is needed (larger batch so prefill is long
  enough to absorb the overhead; or true CacheBlend which replaces prefill
  with attention-recompute — precompute makes this tractable).
- The F1=0.508 layered path is a RoPE-timing anomaly (device-resident uses
  the same default-stream RoPE as SYNC and got 0.447 — between the two).
